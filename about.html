Over a century of commitment to excellence.

Established in 1916, Kalimark began as a humble endeavour crafting beverages that soon became synonymous with quality and taste. With over a century of commitment to excellence, our journey embodies a rich legacy of crafting iconic refreshments loved by millions worldwide.

Identity

Kalimark, a beacon since 1916, embarked on a luminous journey from Virudhupatti, now Virudhunagar. The brand's roots deepened, and its popularity soared. The emblem transcended borders, casting a vibrant glow across the globe.

The torch was passed to the younger generation, propelling the business to new heights. In 1959, cities like Chennai, Tirunelveli and Madurai savoured the renowned Bovonto, a soft beverage that laid the foundation for Kalimark's soft drink supremacy. Since then, Kalimark has stood firm, with Bovonto marking a pivotal moment in its history.

The journey persists, with grandsons and great-grandsons steering Kalimark to new frontiers across Tamil Nadu. Over the past decade, Kalimark has amassed numerous achievements, securing a robust market presence. With a state-of-the-art manufacturing unit, Kalimark consistently delivers remarkable output. The PET bottles emanating from the plant not only quench thirst but also foster trust among customers. The saga continues, with Kalimark leaving an indelible mark on the beverage landscape.

Our history

Kalimark's story began with a vision to create beverages that not only quench thirst but also leave an indelible mark on taste buds. Over the years, we have evolved, adapted and innovated, always staying true to our roots while embracing the dynamic palate of our diverse audience.

From our iconic sodas to a diverse range of refreshing concoctions, each sip encapsulates the essence of tradition and quality that defines Kalimark. Our commitment to using the finest ingredients and adhering to time-honoured recipes has earned us the trust and loyalty of generations of consumers.

As we stride into the future, we continue to blend innovation with our heritage, ensuring that every bottle of Kalimark not only tells a story but becomes a part of yours. Join us in celebrating over a century of taste, tradition and togetherness – because at Kalimark, our history is as vibrant as the flavours we create.
